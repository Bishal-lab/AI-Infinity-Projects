# Sample Insight Reports

Worked examples showing the shape of output the pipeline produces, computed directly against
the seed dataset in [`../sql/seed_data.sql`](../sql/seed_data.sql). These are illustrative —
generated by running the actual aggregations offline (no live n8n/LLM in this environment) — but
the SQL, numbers, and chart config are exactly what the seed data yields, so they double as an
expected-output reference when you test the real pipeline.

---

## 1. "Show me leads by region"

**Generated SQL** (`Generate SQL` node):
```sql
SELECT region, SUM(leads) AS total_leads
FROM sales_marketing
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY total_leads DESC;
```

**Result rows:**

| region | total_leads |
|---|---|
| North | 14,371 |
| East | 10,845 |
| South | 9,308 |
| West | 8,363 |

**SQL Agent insight summary:**
- **Key insights:** North generates the most leads (14,371), roughly 54% more than the
  lowest-performing region, West (8,363).
- **Summary of trends:** Lead volume is fairly evenly distributed across East, South, and West,
  with North as a clear standout.
- **Anomalies:** None significant — no region is collapsing to near-zero volume.
- **Recommended actions:** Investigate what's driving North's lead volume (channel mix, campaign
  timing) and consider reallocating some East/West budget toward tactics that are working in North.
- **Executive summary:** "North leads all regions with 14,371 leads, about 37% more than the
  three-region average of the rest."

**Chart required?** YES (explicit "by region" comparison) → `bar` chart.

**Chart URL:**
```
https://quickchart.io/chart?c={type:'bar',data:{labels:['North','East','South','West'],datasets:[{label:'total_leads',data:[14371,10845,9308,8363]}]}}
```

## 2. "Which campaign performed best?"

**Generated SQL:**
```sql
SELECT campaign_name,
       SUM(sales_revenue) / SUM(spend) AS roas,
       SUM(sales_revenue) AS total_revenue,
       SUM(spend) AS total_spend
FROM sales_marketing
GROUP BY campaign_name
ORDER BY roas DESC
LIMIT 5;
```

**Result rows:**

| campaign_name | roas | total_revenue | total_spend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Influencer Mega Launch | 3.35 | 144,375.65 | 43,143.44 |
| Google Performance Max | 3.29 | 126,525.45 | 38,468.04 |
| Google Shopping Ads | 2.99 | 87,387.72 | 29,253.12 |
| Influencer Wave | 2.84 | 72,899.59 | 25,640.53 |
| TikTok Creator Collab | 2.81 | 85,513.05 | 30,419.87 |

**SQL Agent insight summary:**
- **Key insights:** "Influencer Mega Launch" is the top performer at 3.35x ROAS, narrowly ahead of
  "Google Performance Max" at 3.29x.
- **Summary of trends:** The top 3 campaigns by ROAS are all Search Ads or Influencer campaign
  types — both outperform the Email and generic Social channels on return efficiency.
- **Anomalies:** None — the top-5 ROAS values form a smooth gradient (3.35 → 2.81), no single
  outlier skewing the ranking.
- **Recommended actions:** Shift incremental budget toward Influencer and Google Search Ads
  campaign types; consider scaling "Influencer Mega Launch" specifically given its lead in both
  ROAS and absolute revenue.
- **Executive summary:** "Influencer Mega Launch is the best-performing campaign, returning $3.35
  for every $1 spent."

**Chart required?** YES ("which... performed best" implies ranking/comparison) → `bar` chart.

**Chart URL:**
```
https://quickchart.io/chart?c={type:'bar',data:{labels:['Influencer Mega Launch','Google Performance Max','Google Shopping Ads','Influencer Wave','TikTok Creator Collab'],datasets:[{label:'roas',data:[3.35,3.29,2.99,2.84,2.81]}]}}
```

## 3. "Show me spend vs conversions by channel"

**Generated SQL:**
```sql
SELECT channel, SUM(spend) AS total_spend, SUM(conversions) AS total_conversions
FROM sales_marketing
GROUP BY channel
ORDER BY total_spend DESC;
```

**Result rows:**

| channel | total_spend | total_conversions | cost/conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google | 154,796.05 | 2,352 | 65.81 |
| Instagram | 90,513.60 | 1,286 | 70.38 |
| TikTok | 90,071.33 | 1,500 | 60.05 |
| LinkedIn | 66,565.65 | 1,111 | 59.92 |
| Facebook | 62,179.19 | 1,026 | 60.60 |
| Email | 26,206.96 | 373 | 70.26 |

**SQL Agent insight summary:**
- **Key insights:** Google absorbs the largest share of spend (~32% of total) and delivers the
  most conversions, but LinkedIn is the most cost-efficient channel at $59.92 per conversion.
- **Anomalies:** Instagram and Email both run ~$70/conversion despite very different spend
  scales — worth a closer creative/targeting review on both.
- **Recommended actions:** Test shifting a portion of Instagram spend toward LinkedIn/TikTok,
  which convert more cheaply; keep Google's budget stable given its conversion volume.
- **Executive summary:** "LinkedIn is the most cost-efficient channel at $59.92 per conversion,
  while Google drives the highest total conversion volume."

**Chart required?** YES → `bar` chart (two-series comparison across channels).

**SQL Agent insight summary:**

  repeating the dip; the May-August stabilization suggests the recovery held.

## 5. "Show the sales number market wise"

"Market" maps to the `region` column (North / South / East / West), same dimension used in
example 1's lead breakdown — this question asks for the same cut, but on revenue instead of
lead volume.

**Generated SQL:**
```sql
SELECT region,
       SUM(sales_revenue) AS total_revenue,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(sales_revenue) / SUM(SUM(sales_revenue)) OVER (), 1) AS pct_of_total
FROM sales_marketing
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
```

**Result rows:**

| region (market) | total_revenue | pct_of_total |
|---|---|---|
| North | 383,944.13 | 29.7% |
| East | 340,853.99 | 26.4% |
| South | 298,331.96 | 23.1% |
| West | 268,217.72 | 20.8% |

**SQL Agent insight summary:**
- **Key insights:** North is the top-selling market at $383.9k (29.7% of total revenue), followed
  closely by East at $340.9k (26.4%); West trails at $268.2k (20.8%).
- **Summary of trends:** The North-East-South-West ordering matches the lead-volume ranking from
  "leads by region," confirming lead volume is translating proportionally into revenue rather
  than one region converting disproportionately better or worse.
- **Anomalies:** None — ROAS is tight across all four markets (2.58x-2.68x), so the revenue gap is
  driven by volume, not efficiency differences.
- **Recommended actions:** Since conversion efficiency is roughly even across markets, growing
  West and South's revenue is a volume problem — increase spend/lead-gen activity there rather
  than optimizing conversion rate; North's approach is worth documenting as the volume playbook
  for the other regions.
- **Executive summary:** "North is the top market at $383.9k in sales (29.7% of total), roughly
  43% ahead of the lowest market, West, at $268.2k."

**Chart required?** YES ("market wise" implies a by-dimension breakdown) → `pie` or `bar` chart
(pie/doughnut chosen here since the insight is framed as a % share of total revenue).

**Chart URL:**
```
https://quickchart.io/chart?c={type:'doughnut',data:{labels:['North','East','South','West'],datasets:[{data:[383944.13,340853.99,298331.96,268217.72]}]}}
```
